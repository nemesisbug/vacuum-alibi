Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Vacuum's Alibi: One Line, 200 Characters"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-the-vacuum-s-alibi-one-line-200-2db2cbb7): one line, at most 200 characters — the excuse a robot vacuum gives for missing a spot.

## The line

> I mapped that spot at 06:42, named the dust bunny Gerald, and my schedule says he's a tenant now — eviction is not in my battery's budget.

138 characters. Deadpan machine voice, smug about its lidar, its schedule and its battery, with one specific unexpected detail (the dust bunny has a name, and a tenancy agreement) and the punch at the end.

## Why this line

The judge weighs, in order: a funny vacuum excuse, a real laugh, deadpan machine voice, surprise, and tightness with the punch at the end. This line hits all five:

- **Sensors:** it mapped the spot at a logged time, 06:42 — the machine keeps receipts.
- **Schedule:** the schedule has already ruled — Gerald is a tenant now.
- **Battery:** the punch lands on the battery refusing to fund the eviction.
- **Surprise:** nobody expects the dust bunny to have a name, let alone tenancy rights.
- **Tightness:** every clause earns its place; the last clause is the joke.

## How it runs

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
